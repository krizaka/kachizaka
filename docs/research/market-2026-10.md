# Kachizaka — market research, October 2026

Scope: free prediction games ("pronostics"), their loops, and where Kachizaka (points only, sealed rules, magpie) can be
clearly better. Written 2026-10-17. Every claim below cites a source; what could not be verified is marked **unverified**.

## 1. The references

### Scorecast (France) — the one to beat
Sources: [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/app/id507883973) · [scorecast.fr](https://www.scorecast.fr)

- **What it is**: a free "next-generation sports predictions" app by IDALGO. Tagline "Sports predictions, no money." FR/EN/ES, iOS, Android, web.
- **Sports**: football, rugby, basketball, tennis, handball, table tennis, volleyball, F1/MotoGP, golf, cycling. Hockey is **not** listed.
- **Game loop**: predict scores before kick-off; private contests with friends (invite + group chat); public contests of ~50 players
  of similar level; weekly divisions (at least three predictions a week to qualify); a global leaderboard; daily missions; highlighted
  matches worth double points.
- **Social**: sport rooms chat, friends, call-outs, 3D customisable avatar.
- **Economy**: *Scorecoins* (virtual currency) earned by missions/badges and spent to edit a pick, see community trends, see a friend's
  pick, customise the avatar.
- **Monetisation**: ads + **Scorecast Premium** (no ads, extras), a Premium Competition Pass, avatar packs and skins; a B2B offer
  (Scorecast Business). The listing says it "contains advertising".
- **Weak points**: 3.0/5 on 5 ratings in the App Store listing (tiny sample); a reviewer reports slow result loading after matches
  (the developer blames its servers). Picks editing and "see a friend's pick" sit behind a currency: the fair-play question is
  turned into a shop. Mission/coin/division mechanics are engagement loops rather than a table you can explain.

### Mon Petit Prono (MPP, France)
Sources: [Journal du Geek guide](https://www.journaldugeek.com/dossier/coupe-du-monde-2026-les-meilleurs-conseils-pour-remporter-sa-ligue-mpp/) ·
[90min interview with a co-founder (2021)](https://www.90min.com/fr/posts/interview-martin-jaglin-co-fondateur-de-monpetitprono-revient-sur-le-succes-de-l-appli-pendant-l-euro)

- Launched 2014; ~850k users at the 2018 World Cup, ~1.1M at Euro 2020. Free, no real money; ads shown when the app opens (the company
  says it refuses betting-company partnerships).
- **Loop**: predict the score of each match; a pick counts in all your leagues; private leagues (brands ran leagues of ~10,000 people:
  KFC, VNB, Swile); a league can span one matchday, several weeks or a season.
- **Scoring**: points derive from **bookmaker-style odds** ("odds come from sports-betting experts"; a 68-odds result paid 272 points);
  an exact-score bonus that depends on how rare the score is among players (+20 above 30% of players … +100 for the top 0.5%);
  one ×2 boost; pre-tournament picks (winner, top scorer).
- **Weak points**: the points come from odds, so the *rules* depend on data players cannot audit, and the look is that of a betting
  product even without money; the exact-score bonus moves with other players' picks (your points change after you play).
  Football-centred (rugby added). Ad-funded.

### Superbru
Sources: [App Store, Superbru Football](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/superbru-football/id6450757084) · [superbru.com](https://www.superbru.com/)

- Free score predictor with private pools (no player limit stated) and global leaderboards; Premier League version: 1 pt right
  outcome, 1.5 close score, 3 exact score. Premium at $3.49 (ad-free, predictions data, previews, extra leagues). The listing's age
  rating mentions "Simulated Gambling" and device identifiers used for third-party advertising/tracking; prize pools/jackpots exist
  alongside the free game.
- Weak: soccer-centric in the app, ads + tracking, and a gambling-adjacent rating.

### Underdog Pick'em / Sleeper Picks (UX only)
Sources: [Sacra on Underdog](https://sacra.com/research/underdog-fantasy) · [CBS Sports explainer](https://www.cbssports.com/general/news/how-to-play-daily-fantasy-pickem-on-apps-like-pick6-prizepicks-sleeper-fantasy-underdog)

- Real-money "higher/lower" entries with fixed payouts. We take **nothing** of the money model and nothing of the look. What is worth
  learning as UX: one-tap picks, a slip that shows what you chose before you confirm, and fast mobile flows.
- **ESPN / NHL pick'em and bracket games**: unverified in this pass (no usable source retrieved). Known only as free bracket-style
  games run by the leagues themselves; not used as a claim anywhere.

## 3. Where Kachizaka can be clearly better

1. **Fairness you can read.** Rules are a small data document, **sealed at kick-off** (SHA-256 of the canonical JSON; the database
   refuses edits), and every point shows the rule that earned it. Nobody's points change after the whistle because other people
   picked differently, and no hidden odds feed decides the value of a pick. On the page the seal is a plain « Rules sealed » with a
   details expander, not a raw hash.
2. **Several sports in one league.** One league, one table: the Habs on Tuesday, CF Montréal on Saturday, the Raptors in between.
   Each sport has its own sealed rule set (soccer: exact score / outcome / goal difference; NHL: winner + OT/SO + goal difference;
   NBA: winner + margin band).
3. **Québec and Canada first.** NHL (Canadiens, plus the other Canadian clubs), CF Montréal and MLS, the Raptors, and the Alouettes
   (CFL, later). French first-class, in Québec French with « tu ».
4. **Privacy and no dark patterns.** No ads, no trackers, no currency to peek at a friend's pick, no streak-shaming, no confetti
   slot-machine look. Data stays in Canada (see the privacy page).
5. **Calm, premium, magpie.** The magpie "keeps the score": a table you can trust, not a casino.
6. **Open source.** The scorer and database rules are public (Apache-2.0); nobody can say the scoring is a black box.

Honest limits: Kachizaka is in development, sign-up is closed, there is no mobile store app yet, and the incumbents have years of
users. The pitch is a better *league* for a group of friends, not a bigger crowd.

Rules for the ingestion: server-side cache only, never a client call; no scraping against a site's terms; one adapter per provider
behind `@kachizaka/mcp`'s connector contract; the provider and its terms recorded next to each competition; if a provider's terms
forbid the use, the competition is not offered. **Facts that cost money (live data) are not claimed**: the table refreshes honestly
("updated 2 min ago"), never "live".
